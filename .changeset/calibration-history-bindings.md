Add direct calibration history bindings for constructed, fixed, and enumerated histories. Bound calibrations validate their selected clean run and actual reach, then require the designated law to reject the mutant, without searching other histories. Constructed calibrations use a stable name-derived seed and fresh builds and resolved plans for each driver execution.

Add `probeHistories` to bind automatic forced-probe controls, with inference from the unique construction entering a state. Unbound calibrations retain search during migration and warn at registration; consumers must migrate before search is removed ahead of the first npm release.
