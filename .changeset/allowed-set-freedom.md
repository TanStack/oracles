Add `notAllowed(checkpoint, law, actual, allowed, freedom)`, the allowed-set comparison. It is a violation of `law` at `checkpoint` when `actual` is the same data as no member of `allowed` (the equality `differs` uses), with `{ actual, expected: allowed }` attached, and undefined otherwise, so it chains as `differs` does. `freedom` names the choice the set rests on, in the words of the law's `limits`. When a property's check reaches the call with a freedom that is blank, or that `limits` does not contain verbatim, it throws an `OracleFreedomError` naming the property, the checkpoint, and the freedom; a test whose check lets it escape fails, and the file's other properties still run. Outside the check (in the model, `predict`, `reached`, or the driver) no law is read. Only the freedom's text is checked: keeping the set's members within the stated limits is the author's job, as `docs/model-styles.md` (section 6) and the design skill now say, with a calibration at each freedom's edge.
